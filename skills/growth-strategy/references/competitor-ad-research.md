
# Competitor Ad Research

## Goal

A structured swipe file of competitor ads and a short list of angles and gaps the user can test, without copying anyone's creative.

## Before you start: gather context

- The user's product and 3–10 competitor brands (or a niche to search)
- Platforms to cover: Meta, TikTok, Google
- Market: US by default

## Where to look

- **Meta Ad Library**: all active ads for a page, filter by country = United States
- **TikTok Creative Center / Top Ads**: high-performing ads by industry and region
- **Google Ads Transparency Center**: search, display and YouTube ads by advertiser
- Competitor landing pages, product pages and review sections

## Workflow

1. **List competitors**, including at least two that are bigger than the user and two at a similar stage.
2. **Pull active ads** for each competitor and record them in the swipe table.
3. **Read the signals**:
   - Longevity: an ad running 30+ days is usually profitable enough to keep
   - Variations: many versions of one concept means the concept is a winner they are iterating on
   - Format mix: video vs static vs carousel
4. **Break each ad down** into hook, angle, offer, proof, CTA and landing page.
5. **Group by angle** (pain, outcome, social proof, comparison, demo, founder story, etc.) and count how often each appears.
6. **Find gaps**: angles nobody uses, customer complaints in reviews nobody addresses, audiences nobody speaks to.

## Rules of thumb

- Extract patterns, never copy. Do not reuse competitors' footage, images, scripts or brand names.
- Weight long-running ads more than new ones; new ads may be tests that will be turned off.
- Competitors' 1–3 star reviews are the best source of angles: they show what customers wish was different.

## Output format

1. Swipe table: brand, platform, format, hook (first line or first 3 seconds), angle, offer, CTA, landing page type, days running, notes
2. Angle frequency summary
3. Five gap opportunities, each with a suggested hook
4. Hand-off: recommend `ad-copy` or `ugc-video` (`references/ugc-video-scripts.md`) to turn gaps into creatives
