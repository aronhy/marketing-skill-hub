# TikTok Creative Calendar & Concept Bank

## Weekly production cadence (small team)

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Mon | Review last week's metrics; pick 2 winners to iterate and 2 new concepts |
| Tue | Write scripts and 5 conflict hooks per script (`ugc-video` (`references/ugc-video-scripts.md`)) |
| Wed | Brief creators / film in-house |
| Thu | Edit: 3–5 hook variants per body, subtitles, 9:16 |
| Fri | Launch new batch into the testing campaign |
| Daily | Reply to comments on Spark Ads; note new objections for next week's scripts |

Target: 4–8 new ads per week; refresh any winner showing falling CTR and rising frequency.

## Concept bank (fictional CoreSeat cushion)

| # | Concept | Conflict hook (first 3 s) | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flat cushion fail | Creator presses a cheap foam pad flat with one finger: "I paid $30 for THIS?" | Talking head + demo |
| 2 | Partner vs purchase | Partner off-camera: "You bought ANOTHER cushion?" Creator: "Watch this." | Skit |
| 3 | 6 pm test | Creator groaning standing up at 6 pm, then cut to "same chair, two weeks later" | Day-in-the-life |
| 4 | Reply to a doubter | Green-screen a comment "these never last" → "It's been 11 months, look." | Reply video |
| 5 | Road trip stakes | "Six-hour drive, my back gives out at hour two. Not this time." | POV in car |

## Spark Ads workflow

1. Creator posts the video organically on their account.
2. Creator generates an authorization code for the post and sends it.
3. Add the code in Ads Manager and run it as an ad.
4. Agree in the contract on the authorization duration and paid usage fee.

## Kill / iterate thresholds (set from the account's own averages)

| Metric | Kill if… | Iterate if… |
|---|---|---|
| 2 s view rate | Well below account average after meaningful impressions | Average, but 6 s rate drops sharply → fix the second beat |
| CTR | Below average with strong view rates → weak middle/CTA | Near average → test new CTA or offer line |
| CPA | > 2× target after enough spend | 1.2–1.5× target with strong early metrics |
