# Hook Bank & Native English Fixes

## Hook templates by angle

Fill the brackets with the user's real facts. Never invent numbers or reviews.

| Angle | Templates |
|---|---|
| PAIN | "If [problem] hits you every [time], this is for you." · "I was done with [problem] after [specific moment]." · "[Problem] isn't normal. Here's what fixed it for me." |
| OUT | "Imagine [desired moment] without [problem]." · "This is what [outcome] actually looks like." |
| SOC | "[Number] people switched to this last month." · "The review that made me try it: '[short real review]'." |
| AUTH | "Why [expert type] recommend [mechanism]." · "Tested by [credible source]: here's what happened." |
| COMP | "[Old way] vs [product]: one of these lasts." · "Stop buying [cheap alternative] every [period]." |
| OBJ | "'Isn't it just another [category]?' Fair question." · "Worried about [objection]? So was I." |
| DEMO | "Watch what happens when [simple test]." · "One [action] and you'll get why this works." |
| STORY | "I built this after [founder problem]." · "My [family member] couldn't [do thing]. So we made this." |
| ID | "For people who [specific behavior]." · "If you [identity trait tied to the product], you need this." |
| VAL | "Less than [cost per day] a day for [benefit]." · "One [product] replaced my [list of items]." |
| RISK | "Try it for 30 days. If it doesn't [outcome], send it back." |
| CUR | "The [category] mistake almost everyone makes." · "Nobody tells you this about [topic]." |
| URG | "Our [real event] ends [real date]." |

Policy reminder: avoid questions that assert personal attributes ("Are you overweight?"). Frame around the product or situation.

## Translated-sounding → native

| Translated-sounding | Native |
|---|---|
| High quality material, best choice for you! | Dense memory foam that doesn't go flat. |
| Our company is a professional manufacturer. | We've been making cushions for 12 years. |
| Dear customer, welcome to buy! | (Delete. Start with the benefit.) |
| Very comfortable and very soft and very good. | Soft enough to sink into, firm enough to support. |
| Suitable for office, car, home, etc. | Works on office chairs, car seats and kitchen stools. |
| Don't hesitate, buy now!!! | Try it for 30 days. |
| It can effectively relieve the pain. | Takes pressure off your lower back. |

Style checks: contractions, second person, one idea per sentence, no stacked adjectives, no random capitals, at most one exclamation mark per ad.
