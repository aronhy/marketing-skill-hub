# Keywords, Negatives & RSA Example

## Starter negative keyword list

Review and adapt; not every term applies to every business.

| Group | Terms |
|---|---|
| Free / cheap intent | free, freebie, giveaway, coupon code (if no codes), cheap (if premium), dollar store |
| Jobs and education | job, jobs, career, salary, hiring, course, class, certification, degree |
| DIY and info only | diy, how to make, homemade, pattern, template (if not selling) |
| Second-hand | used, second hand, refurbished (if not selling), rental (if not offered) |
| Wrong buyer | wholesale, bulk, manufacturer, supplier, alibaba (for retail stores); retail terms for B2B |
| Wrong channel | amazon, walmart, ebay, near me (if online only) |
| Irrelevant meanings | add as you find them in the search terms report |

1. Seat Cushion for Back Pain
2. Sit All Day Without Back Ache
3. Memory Foam That Stays Firm
4. 30-Day Comfort Guarantee
5. Ships From the US in 1–2 Days
6. Free Shipping on Every Order
7. Relieve Tailbone Pressure
8. Works on Office & Car Seats
9. Thousands of 5-Star Reviews *(only if true)*
10. Washable, Non-Slip Cover
11. Ergonomic U-Shaped Design
12. Try It Risk-Free for 30 Days
13. Better Posture at Your Desk
14. Shop the CoreSeat Cushion
15. Designed for Long Workdays

Descriptions (≤90 characters each):
1. High-density memory foam that keeps its shape. Feel the difference by the end of day one.
2. U-shaped cutout takes pressure off your tailbone. Non-slip base stays put on any chair.
3. Not comfier in 30 days? Send it back. Free US shipping, delivered in 3–5 business days.
4. Remote workers and drivers use CoreSeat to sit longer with less back strain. Order today.

Count characters before publishing; limits are enforced by Google Ads.
