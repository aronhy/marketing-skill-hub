
# Ad Policy Compliance

## Goal

Ads and accounts that stay running because they follow the platforms' rules, plus a calm, factual process when something gets flagged.

## Scope and hard limits

This skill helps the user comply. It does not help evade enforcement. Decline and explain why if asked about cloaking (showing reviewers a different page from users), buying, renting or farming ad accounts, using someone else's identity or business documents, or creating new accounts to get around a ban. These practices violate platform terms and usually end in permanent bans.

## Before you start: gather context

- Product and category
- Platforms used
- The rejected ad or restriction notice (exact wording), if any
- Website URL and what policies are on it

## Pre-launch checklist

**Business and account**
- Business details (name, address, website) consistent across the ad account, payment method and website
- Business verification completed where available
- Two-factor authentication on all admin accounts
- Reliable payment method; new accounts increase spend gradually

**Website**
- Clear product information, real prices, accurate shipping times
- Privacy policy, terms, refund and return policy, shipping policy, contact page with a real email (and address where expected)
- Landing page matches the ad's claims and offer
- No pop-ups that block content, no broken checkout

**Ad content**
- No claims the product cannot back up ("cure", "guaranteed results", "#1" without proof)
- No "personal attributes" targeting language (for example "Are you overweight?" or "Struggling with debt?"); talk about the product, not the viewer's traits
- Before/after imagery avoided in health, weight loss and cosmetic categories
- No fake buttons, fake notifications or misleading urgency
- Prices and discounts accurate and available on the landing page
- No unauthorized use of trademarks, celebrities or copyrighted music and footage

## Higher-risk categories

Supplements, weight loss, cosmetics with medical claims, CBD and hemp, alcohol, financial products, dating, gambling, adult products and weapons-related items face extra rules, certifications or outright bans that vary by platform and country. Check each platform's current policy pages before spending.

## When something gets flagged

1. Read the exact reason. Identify the specific policy.
2. Fix the cause in the ad or landing page if the flag is valid; do not just resubmit unchanged.
3. If the flag is a mistake, request a review through the official channel with a short, factual explanation.
4. For account-level restrictions, complete any requested verification and wait for the review; avoid launching workarounds that make the case worse.
5. Keep a log of rejections, causes and fixes to prevent repeats.

## Output format

1. Compliance checklist with pass / fix for each item
2. Risky phrases in the user's copy, each with a compliant rewrite
3. Category-specific warnings
4. If flagged: likely cause, fix, and appeal text draft

Policies change frequently; always point the user to the platform's current advertising policy pages for final confirmation.
