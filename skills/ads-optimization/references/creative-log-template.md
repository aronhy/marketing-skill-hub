## Monthly pattern review questions

1. Which angles produced winners most often?
2. Did conflict hooks beat non-conflict hooks on hook rate and CPA?
3. Which formats and creators won?
4. How long did winners last before fatigue?
5. What will we test more of next month, and what will we stop?
