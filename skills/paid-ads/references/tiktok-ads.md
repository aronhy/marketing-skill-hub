
# TikTok Ads

## Goal

A TikTok ads setup built around a steady supply of native-looking creatives, with tracking that works and rules for testing and refreshing.

## Before you start: gather context

- Product, price, target customer age; is the product easy to demonstrate on video?
- Budget and target CPA
- Tracking: TikTok Pixel plus Events API? (If not, run `references/tracking-setup.md`.)
- Selling on TikTok Shop, own website, or both
- Creative capacity: how many new videos per week, in-house or creators?

## Principles

- **Creative is the strategy.** On TikTok, ads must look like TikTok content: vertical 9:16, people talking to camera, real settings, captions on screen, sound on.
- **The first 2–3 seconds decide everything.** Lead with the hook, never with the logo.
- **Volume beats polish.** Plan for several new creatives per week; TikTok creatives fatigue faster than on Meta, often within one to two weeks.

## Campaign setup

- Start broad; let the algorithm find buyers from creative signals.
- Use automated campaign types (such as Smart+) or a simple structure: one testing campaign and one scaling campaign.
- Check minimum budgets per ad group in the current interface; underfunded ad groups never exit learning.
- **Spark Ads**: run ads through organic posts from your own account or from creators (with their authorization code). They keep social proof (likes, comments) and usually look more native.
- For TikTok Shop, use the shop-specific campaign types and make sure the product listing, price and reviews are strong first.

## Creative formats that work

- Talking-head problem → solution
- Unboxing and first impressions
- "3 reasons I switched to…"
- Green-screen reaction to a review or comment
- Before / during / after demonstration (avoid policy-sensitive before/after claims)
- Reply-to-comment videos addressing objections
Use `ugc-video` (`references/ugc-video-scripts.md`) to write them and `ugc-video` (`references/influencer-outreach.md`) to source creators.

## Metrics to watch

- Hook: 2-second and 6-second view rates
- Hold: average watch time, completion rate
- Click: CTR
- Conversion: CVR, CPA, ROAS
A high hook rate with low CTR means the middle or CTA is weak; a low hook rate means change the opening.

## Rules of thumb

- Refresh creatives on a schedule, not only when performance crashes.
- Reuse winning bodies with new hooks; test five hooks on one proven video.
- Only use music from TikTok's commercial music library in ads.
- Comments are public and visible on Spark Ads; moderate them and reply to objections.

## Common mistakes for sellers new to the US

- Re-using polished TV-style or Meta static ads on TikTok
- Hooks that are too slow or start with the brand
- Using trending sounds that are not licensed for commercial use
- Copy that sounds translated; use creators who speak naturally to US viewers

## Output format

1. Campaign plan: campaigns, budgets, optimization event
2. Five creative concepts with hook, format and talking points
3. Weekly creative production and refresh schedule
4. Metric thresholds for keeping, iterating or killing a creative

TikTok's ad products and policies change frequently; confirm current options in TikTok Ads Manager.
