# Positioning & Offer: Worked Example

Fictional product: **CoreSeat** ergonomic seat cushion, $49.

## Positioning statement

For remote workers and drivers who get lower-back pain after long hours of sitting, CoreSeat is the seat cushion that keeps your posture supported all day, unlike flat foam pads that flatten within weeks, because its high-density memory foam holds its shape and is backed by a 30-day comfort guarantee.

## Feature → outcome

| Feature | Outcome the customer feels |
|---|---|
| High-density memory foam | Still supportive at 6 pm, not just at 9 am |
| U-shaped cutout | Less pressure on the tailbone |
| Non-slip base | Stays in place on office and car seats |
| Washable cover | Fresh without buying a new one |

## Three offers to test

| Offer | Price | Included | Expected effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Straight | $49, free shipping | 1 cushion | Baseline conversion rate |
| B. Bundle | $79 | Cushion + lumbar pillow | Higher AOV, better CPA tolerance |
| C. Risk reversal | $49 | 1 cushion + 30-day "sit on it" guarantee highlighted everywhere | Higher conversion rate for cold traffic |

## Objection map

| Objection | Proof | Where |
|---|---|---|
| "Will it actually help my back?" | Reviews mentioning back relief, demo of pressure distribution | Ad, above the fold |
| "Foam cushions go flat." | 12-month use review, density spec explained simply | Page FAQ, ad comparison angle |
| "Is it too firm / too soft?" | Firmness description, 30-day guarantee | Page, abandoned-cart email |
| "Shipping takes forever." | "Ships from the US in 1–2 days, arrives in 3–5" | Ad, page, checkout |
| "Never heard of this brand." | Review count, real photos, About page, contact info | Page, post-purchase emails |
