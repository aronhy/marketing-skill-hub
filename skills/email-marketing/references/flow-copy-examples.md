# Flow Copy Examples

Fictional brand: **CoreSeat** ($49 seat cushion). Adapt the voice to the user's brand.

## Welcome flow

**Email 1 (immediately)**
- Subject: Your 10% code is inside
- Preview: Plus the one thing most people get wrong about sitting
- Body:
  > Thanks for joining. Here's your code: **WELCOME10** (valid 7 days).
  >
  > Quick truth: most back strain from sitting isn't about your chair. It's about pressure building in the same spots for hours. CoreSeat spreads that pressure out, so 3 pm feels like 10 am.
  >
  > [Shop CoreSeat]
- CTA: Shop CoreSeat

**Email 2 (Day 2)**: social proof
- Subject: "I stopped doing the stand-up groan"
- Body: three short real reviews with photos, link to the product

**Email 3 (Day 4)**: objection
- Subject: Will it go flat?
- Body: explain the foam density simply, show a long-term customer photo, remind of the 30-day guarantee

**Email 4 (Day 6)**: last call on the code
- Subject: Your 10% code expires tomorrow
- Body: short reminder, one image, one button

## Abandoned checkout flow

**Email 1 (1 hour)**
- Subject: Your CoreSeat is still in your cart
- Body: product image, price, "Ships in 1–2 days from the US", button back to checkout

**Email 2 (24 hours)**
- Subject: Any questions before you sit on it?
- Body: 3 FAQs (firmness, fit, returns), reply-to-ask line from a real person

**Email 3 (48–72 hours)** *(optional incentive)*
- Subject: Free shipping + 30 days to decide
- Body: risk reversal first; only add a discount if margins allow

## Post-purchase flow

| Email | Timing | Subject | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Order placed | Thanks! Here's what happens next | Shipping timeline, support contact |
| 2 | Delivered + 1 day | How to get the most from your CoreSeat | Setup tips: position, chair height, cover washing |
| 3 | Delivered + 14 days | How's your back feeling? | Review request with photo upload link |
| 4 | Delivered + 30 days | For your car, too | Cross-sell: second cushion or lumbar pillow bundle |

## Win-back (no purchase in 120 days, for repeatable products)

- Subject: It's been a while
- Body: what's new, best-selling bundle, light offer; one CTA
