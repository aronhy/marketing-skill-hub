# Google Ads

## Goal

A Google Ads account that captures existing demand profitably, with clean conversion tracking, a sensible campaign mix and a weekly optimization routine.

## Before you start: gather context

- Business type: ecommerce or lead generation; product catalog size
- Target CPA or ROAS, budget
- Conversion tracking status: Google tag, enhanced conversions, GA4 link (if missing, run `tracking-setup`)
- Merchant Center status for ecommerce: feed approved? any suspensions?
- Brand search volume: do people already search the brand name?

## Choose campaign types

| Situation | Start with |
|---|---|
| Ecommerce with a product feed | Shopping or Performance Max with a well-optimized feed |
| People search for the problem or category | Search, non-brand |
| Brand already gets searches | Search, brand (cheap, protects against competitors) |
| Need awareness or creative reach | Demand Gen or YouTube, after search is working |

## Search campaigns

1. **Keyword research by intent**: buying intent ("buy", "best", "near me", product + model), problem intent, comparison intent ("X vs Y", "X alternative").
2. **Group tightly**: one ad group per theme so ads and landing pages match the query.
3. **Match types**: use broad match only with smart bidding and strong negatives; use phrase and exact for control on small budgets.
4. **Negatives from day one**: free, jobs, DIY, used, cheap (if premium), wholesale (if retail), irrelevant meanings.
5. **Responsive search ads**: up to 15 headlines (30 characters) and 4 descriptions (90 characters). Cover keyword, benefit, proof, offer and CTA. Pin only when required. Use `ad-copy-angles` for variants.
6. **Assets**: sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, images, price where relevant.

## Shopping and Performance Max

- Feed quality decides results: descriptive titles (brand + product + key attribute + size/color), GTINs where they exist, high-quality images, accurate price and availability.
- Merchant Center needs clear shipping and return policies, contact information and a trustworthy site; misrepresentation suspensions are common for new overseas stores.
- In PMax, separate asset groups by product category or margin tier; add brand exclusions so PMax does not take credit for brand searches.

## Bidding

- Start with Maximize Conversions (or Maximize Conversion Value) to gather data.
- Move to target CPA or target ROAS once the campaign has a steady flow of conversions (roughly 30 or more in 30 days).
- Change targets in small steps (10–20%) and wait a week between changes.

## Weekly optimization routine

- Search terms report: add negatives, promote high performers to their own keywords
- Check impression share lost to budget vs lost to rank
- Pause keywords and products with spend and no conversions beyond 2× target CPA
- Review asset performance in RSAs and PMax; replace weak assets
- Compare landing page conversion rates per ad group

## Common mistakes for sellers new to the US

- Running broad match with no negatives and manual bids
- Sending all traffic to the homepage instead of matching pages
- Ignoring Merchant Center policy requirements until the account is suspended
- Letting PMax spend on brand terms and reporting it as new growth

## Output format

1. Recommended campaign mix with budget split
2. Keyword table: keyword, match type, intent, ad group
3. Negative keyword list
4. RSA assets: 15 headlines, 4 descriptions per ad group
5. Feed fixes (ecommerce)
6. Weekly optimization checklist

Confirm current character limits and campaign options in the Google Ads interface; they change over time.
