
# Landing Page for Overseas Traffic

## Goal

A page that continues the ad's promise, earns the trust of a buyer who has never heard of the brand, and makes buying easy on a phone.

## Before you start: gather context

- The ad(s) sending traffic: angle, hook, offer
- Product, price, offer, proof assets (reviews, photos, guarantees)
- Current page URL and conversion rate, if live
- Platform: Shopify, page builder, custom

## Page structure (mobile first)

1. **Above the fold**: headline that matches the ad's hook, one-line subhead with the main outcome, hero image or short video of the product in use, price or offer, primary CTA, star rating with review count
2. **Problem and outcome**: short, in the customer's words
3. **How it works**: three steps or three key benefits with visuals
4. **Proof**: reviews with photos, UGC, numbers, press or certifications
5. **Comparison**: vs the usual alternative
6. **Offer details**: what's included, bundles, guarantee, shipping time, returns
7. **FAQ**: answer the top objections (shipping, sizing, returns, how to use, materials)
8. **Final CTA** repeating the offer

## Trust signals overseas buyers look for

- Clear, honest delivery estimates and shipping costs before checkout
- Easy returns and a visible guarantee
- Real reviews (never fabricated)
- Contact options and an About page with a real story
- Familiar payment options (major cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Shop Pay where available)
- Clean, natural English; no translated phrasing

## Message match

The first screen should feel like the next sentence after the ad. If the ad leads with "fixes back pain at your desk", the page headline should not be "Welcome to our store."

## CRO checklist

- Loads fast on mobile (aim for under ~3 seconds)
- One primary action; remove menus and distractions on dedicated landing pages
- Sticky add-to-cart button on mobile
- Price, shipping and returns visible without hunting
- Images show scale, detail and real use
- Checkout has no surprise fees

## Rules of thumb

- If CTR is good and CVR is poor, fix the page and offer before touching targeting.
- Test big changes first: headline, hero, offer. Then smaller ones.
- Different angles deserve different pages or at least different above-the-fold sections.

## Output format

1. Section-by-section outline with copy for each section
2. Above-the-fold variations (2–3) for testing
3. Audit of the current page against the CRO checklist (if a page exists): issue, impact, fix
