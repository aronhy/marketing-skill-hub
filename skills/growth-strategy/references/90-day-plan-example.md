# 90-Day Plan: Worked Example

Fictional example used across this library: **CoreSeat**, an ergonomic memory-foam seat cushion for people who sit all day. Sold DTC on Shopify to the US, shipped from a US warehouse.

## Inputs

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Price | $49 |
| Product + packaging + fulfillment + shipping | $14 |
| Payment fees (~3%) | $1.50 |
| Returns allowance (~5%) | $2.50 |
| Gross profit per order | $31 |
| Gross margin | ~63% |
| Break-even ROAS | 1 ÷ 0.63 ≈ 1.6 |
| Break-even CPA | $31 |
| Target CPA (keep ~$10 profit per first order) | ~$21 |
| Monthly ad budget | $6,000 |

## Diagnosis

Visual, easy-to-demo product with an everyday problem (back pain from sitting) and a clear before/after feeling. Impulse-to-considered price point. Primary channel: Meta, with TikTok creators as a creative source. Google Search later for "seat cushion for back pain" style queries.

## Plan

| Phase | Goal | Actions | Budget | KPI | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–7: Foundations | Tracking and site ready | Pixel + CAPI, page with reviews and guarantee, 3 offers drafted | $0 ads | All QA checks pass | `paid-ads` (`references/tracking-setup.md`), `references/landing-page.md`, `references/offer-positioning.md` |
| Days 8–30: Validate | Find 2–3 winning creatives | 4 concepts × 3–5 ads in ABO tests; 10 creator UGC videos | ~$150/day | CPA ≤ $31 on at least one concept | `paid-ads` (`references/meta-ads.md`), `ad-copy`, `ugc-video` (`references/ugc-video-scripts.md`), `ads-optimization` (`references/creative-testing.md`) |
| Days 31–60: Optimize | Reach target CPA | Scale winners 20% every 2–3 days, refresh 4–6 creatives/week, launch email flows | ~$200/day | Blended CPA ≤ $21, MER ≥ 2 | `ads-optimization` (`references/ads-review-optimization.md`), `email-marketing` |
| Days 61–90: Scale | Grow revenue at target | Advantage+ scaling, start Google Search, start TikTok Spark Ads with best creators | ~$250–300/day | Revenue +50% vs month 2 at same CPA | `paid-ads` (`references/google-ads.md`), `paid-ads` (`references/tiktok-ads.md`), `ugc-video` (`references/influencer-outreach.md`) |

## First three actions this week

1. Install Pixel + Conversions API through the official Shopify integration and place a test order.
2. Add the 30-day guarantee, shipping time and review widget above the fold on the product page.
3. Order 10 cushions for creators and brief the first five.

Numbers above are illustrative. Replace them with the user's own costs and prices.
