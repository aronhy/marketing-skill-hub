## Review mining prompts

Run these against competitors' 1–3 star reviews (Amazon, Trustpilot, site reviews, TikTok comments):

1. What did buyers expect that the product did not deliver?
2. Which complaints repeat at least three times?
3. What words do buyers use for the problem? (Use those words in ads.)
4. What would have made them keep the product?

Turn each repeated complaint into a gap angle: "Unlike most [category], ours [solves complaint], and here's proof."
