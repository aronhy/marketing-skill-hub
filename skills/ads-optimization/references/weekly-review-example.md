# Weekly Review: Worked Example

Fictional CoreSeat account, Meta only. Target CPA $21.

## 1. Headline

Spend rose 18% and CPA rose from $19 to $25, driven by creative fatigue on the top ad, not by the site or tracking.

## 2. KPIs

| Metric | This week | Last week | Target | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spend | $1,420 | $1,200 | — | +18% |
| CPM | $16.10 | $15.40 | — | +5% |
| CTR (link) | 1.35% | 1.72% | — | −22% |
| CVR (click → purchase) | 4.4% | 4.5% | — | −2% |
| Purchases | 57 | 63 | — | −10% |
| CPA | $24.90 | $19.00 | $21 | +31% |
| Frequency (top ad) | 3.4 | 2.1 | — | +62% |
| Store orders (all sources) | 81 | 85 | — | −5% |
| MER (store revenue ÷ ad spend) | 2.8 | 3.5 | ≥ 2.5 | −20% |

## 3. What changed

- Budget on the scaling campaign raised twice (20% each)
- No new creatives launched this week
- No site or price changes; tracking QA passed

## 4. Wins

- C017 "Partner vs purchase" still under target at $18 CPA with frequency 1.8

## 5. Losses

- C011 "Flat pad fail" CPA rose from $16 to $29 as frequency climbed past 3

## 6. Diagnosis

CPM up slightly (normal for November). CTR down sharply with frequency up on the top ad → **creative fatigue**. CVR flat → page and offer fine. Tracking fine (store orders consistent).

## 7. Learnings

- Winners in this account fatigue around frequency 3; plan replacements before that point.
- Raising budget without new creatives accelerates fatigue.
