# Risky Phrases → Compliant Rewrites

| Risky | Why it's risky | Compliant rewrite |
|---|---|---|
| "Are you overweight?" | Asserts a personal attribute of the viewer | "A simple way to stay on track with your fitness goals" |
| "Struggling with debt?" | Personal financial attribute | "Tools to help you organize your monthly budget" |
| "Cure your back pain forever" | Medical cure claim, unprovable | "Designed to reduce pressure on your lower back while you sit" |
| "Guaranteed results in 7 days" | Guaranteed outcome | "Many customers notice a difference within the first week" *(only if supported)* |
| "Lose 10 lbs fast" | Weight-loss claim, unrealistic | Avoid; focus on product features and experience |
| "#1 best seat cushion" | Unsubstantiated superlative | "Rated 4.8/5 by 2,000+ customers" *(only if true)* |
| "Doctors hate this trick" | Sensational, misleading | "How memory foam supports better sitting posture" |
| "Only 2 left!" (when untrue) | Fake scarcity | Use only real stock levels or real deadlines |
| "Was $99, now $49" (never sold at $99) | Deceptive reference price | "$49 with free shipping" |
| "FDA approved" (when not) | False regulatory claim | Never claim certifications you don't hold |
| Fake play buttons, fake notifications in images | Misleading design | Use real product imagery |
| Using a celebrity's photo or name | No endorsement or rights | Use your own customers and creators with permission |

## Before/after

In health, weight loss and cosmetic categories, avoid before/after images and implied body comparisons. Show the product in use and the experience instead.

## Landing page mismatch triggers

- Ad promises a discount the page doesn't show
- Ad shows a product variant the page doesn't sell
- Page redirects through multiple domains
- Page content differs by visitor (cloaking): never do this

## Appeal template

> Hello, our ad [ad ID] was rejected under [policy name]. We have reviewed the policy. [Either: The ad does not [violate X] because [specific fact]. / We have updated the ad to remove [element] and the landing page now shows [element].] Our business details are verified and our site includes refund, shipping and contact policies. We'd appreciate a review. Thank you.

Keep appeals short, factual and polite. One appeal per issue.
