## Core rules

- Two or three platforms at most for a beginner; one core piece of content a week, repurposed everywhere.
- The first 3 seconds decide reach: open with conflict (see `ugc-video`).
- Upload clean originals to each platform; content carrying another platform's watermark is recommended less.
- Every post has an exit: profile link, newsletter, DM or product page, tracked with UTMs.
- Judge content by shares, saves, watch time and link clicks, not followers alone.
- Plans must fit the hours the user actually has; fixed weekly quantity, batch filming, no daily treadmill.
- No bought followers, engagement pods, mass account creation or automation that fakes activity; use licensed music only; creators and gifted posts disclose (FTC).
- Platform features, limits and policies change often; tell the user to confirm current rules in each app.
