# Overseas Growth Plan

## Goal

Give the user a clear 90-day plan for acquiring customers in the target market (default: US): which channels, in what order, with what budget, and which skill in this library to use at each step.

## Before you start: gather context

Ask for anything the user has not provided:

- Product, price, gross margin, and whether it is B2C or B2B
- Current stage: idea / first sales / scaling
- Monthly marketing budget (USD) and team size
- Assets already in place: website, store, pixel, email list, social accounts, creatives
- Main goal for the next 90 days (first sales, profitable CPA, scale revenue, leads)

## Workflow

1. **Classify the business.** B2C impulse product, B2C considered purchase, or B2B.
2. **Check the foundations.** If tracking, a trustworthy website or a clear offer is missing, fix those first (`tracking-setup`, `landing-page`, `offer-positioning`).
3. **Pick a primary channel** using the channel-fit table below. Start with one paid channel, not three.
4. **Set unit economics.** Break-even ROAS = 1 / gross margin. Target CPA = gross profit per order × share you are willing to spend.
5. **Split the budget.** Roughly 70% proven channel, 20% creative and audience testing, 10% new channel experiments.
6. **Write the 90-day plan** in three phases: Validate (days 1–30), Optimize (31–60), Scale (61–90).
7. **Route** each task to the matching skill.

## Channel fit

| Situation | Primary channel | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| Visual, impulse B2C product | Meta ads | `meta-ads` |
| People already search for the solution | Google Search / Shopping | `google-ads` |
| Under-35 audience, demo-friendly product | TikTok ads + creators | `tiktok-ads`, `influencer-outreach` |
| B2B, wholesale, distributors, partnerships | Cold email | `cold-email-setup`, `cold-outreach-copy` |
| Have traffic but low repeat purchases | Email flows | `email-lifecycle` |

## Skill map

- Research: `market-research`, `competitor-ad-research`, `offer-positioning`
- Ads: `meta-ads`, `google-ads`, `tiktok-ads`
- Creative: `ad-copy-angles`, `ugc-video-scripts`, `ugc-video-teardown`, `creative-testing`
- Optimize: `ads-review-optimization`
- Foundations: `tracking-setup`, `ad-policy-compliance`, `landing-page`
- Email & outreach: `cold-email-setup`, `cold-outreach-copy`, `email-lifecycle`
- Creators: `influencer-outreach`

## Rules of thumb

- One primary channel until it is profitable. Spreading a small budget across platforms gives no channel enough data.
- No paid ads before tracking works. Every dollar spent without tracking is a dollar you cannot learn from.
- Paid ads amplify an offer; they do not fix a weak one. If CTR is fine but conversion is poor, work on the offer and page.
- Plan for Q4 (Black Friday to Christmas): ad costs rise sharply, so have winning creatives ready by October.

## Output format

1. One-paragraph diagnosis of where the business is
2. Unit economics table: price, gross margin, break-even ROAS, target CPA
3. Channel choice with one-line reasoning
4. 90-day plan table: phase, goal, actions, budget, KPI, skill to use
5. The first three actions for this week
